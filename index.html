Howell CPR & AED Club

Learn CPR. Know the AED. Be ready when it counts.

A student-run club open to every Howell High School student, built with hands-on training from Livingston County EMS. No experience or background required — just a willingness to learn.

How to join What we do

Built for every kind of student

This club isn't just for students already headed into medicine. It's for anyone who wants a skill that matters — no minimum GPA, no prerequisite class, no experience needed. Whether you're in AFJROTC, working toward an automotive or technical certification, planning a future in childcare or education, or you're just curious, there's a place for you here.

We partner directly with Livingston County EMS to run real, hands-on training sessions — the same techniques taught in professional certification courses, adapted for a high school club setting.

Who this is for

  • AFJROTC cadets building leadership & emergency-response skills
  • Automotive & technical program students (roadside & shop safety)
  • Future childcare & education students
  • Anyone who wants a resume- and college-application-ready certification
  • Anyone who just wants to know what to do in an emergency

What you'll learn

Training runs in small hands-on sessions led by Livingston County EMS staff and trained student leads, working up to an official certification.

CPR

Hands-Only and full CPR for adults, children, and infants — chest compressions, rescue breaths, and recognizing cardiac arrest.

AED Use

How to locate, operate, and safely use an automated external defibrillator in an emergency.

Choking Response

Recognizing and responding to a choking emergency in adults, children, and infants.

How to join

No cost to attend an interest meeting, and no commitment required until you're ready.

  1. Come to an interest meeting

    Drop by — no sign-up needed. Meeting details are in the Meetings section below.

  2. Fill out the club sign-up form

    Takes less than five minutes. Ask an officer or your faculty advisor for the current link or QR code.

  3. Attend training sessions

    Hands-on practice with CPR manikins and training AEDs, led by Livingston County EMS instructors and student officers.

  4. Get certified

    Pass a short skills check and written test (80% minimum) to earn an official CPR/AED certification card.
