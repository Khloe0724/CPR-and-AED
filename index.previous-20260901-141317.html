Howell CPR & AED Club

Learn CPR. Know the AED. Save a life when it counts.

A student-run club open to all Howell High School students. Partnered with Livingston County EMS to offer flexible, group-scheduled workshops and certified life-saving skills.

Built around your schedule

We work directly with Livingston County EMS instructors to host practical training workshops. Rather than demanding weekly meeting commitments, workshops are scheduled on dates that fit the enrolled group.

Cost & Certification: Final per-student pricing will be established by Livingston County EMS once enrollment numbers are locked in (typically covering official card and materials costs). We are actively exploring fundraising initiatives to lower or offset out-of-pocket expenses for members!

Who benefits most

  • Healthcare Pathways: Key preparation for nursing, pre-med, or HHS senior EMT programs.
  • AFJROTC Cadets: Satisfies emergency preparedness goals and service resume credentials.
  • Childcare & Education: Meets mandatory Michigan LARA child safety prerequisites.
  • Automotive & Trades: Boosts hiring eligibility with verified shop safety certification.

Career & College Advantages

Gaining formal CPR/AED credentials provides immediate competitive value in job markets and college admissions.

Higher Earning Potential

Qualified candidates often command better pay for positions in lifeguarding, camp counseling, childcare, and healthcare.

College App Standing

Highlights real-world responsibility, leadership, initiative, and community service on applications.

Workplace Readiness

Satisfies core safety requirements across vocational trades, athletic coaching, and emergency services.

What you'll learn

Hands-on practice conducted using equipment provided in partnership with Livingston County EMS.

CPR (Adult, Child, Infant)

High-quality compressions, rescue breathing techniques, and rapid response to cardiac arrest.

AED Operation

Safe operation and effective deployment of Automated External Defibrillators in public environments.

Choking Emergency

Abdominal thrusts and choking management for adults, children, and infants.

Student Leadership Roles

Stand out on applications by taking charge. We offer dedicated student leadership roles, and more executive positions will open as membership grows!

Workshop & Training Lead

Assist LCEMS personnel during workshops, help manage practice gear, and lead peer drill groups.

Outreach & Promotions Lead

Direct student recruitment, manage school announcements, and run community publicity.

Operations & Schedule Lead

Coordinate flexible workshop dates, handle enrollment tracking, and manage communication schedules.
